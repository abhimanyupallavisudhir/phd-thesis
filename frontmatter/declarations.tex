\section*{Published and collaborative work}

\begin{draftblock}
Parts of this thesis have been published, or are under review, as follows. Where work was done in collaboration, my individual contribution is indicated.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:market-rl}} is based on: Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir and Long Tran-Thanh, ``Market-based architectures in RL and beyond''.
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:agency}} is based on the working articles ``Price recursion is the rational theory of reward'' and ``Reward as hyperstitional information'', and the accompanying \texttt{marketgrad} library.
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:infonomy}} is based on: Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir and Long Tran-Thanh, ``Extrapolating Volition with Recursive Information Markets''.
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:solib}} is based on: Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir, Arjun Panickssery and Jackson Kaunismaa, ``A Benchmark for Scalable Oversight Mechanisms''.
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:nonvf}} is based on: Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir and Long Tran-Thanh, ``Betting on what is neither verifiable nor falsifiable'' (arXiv:2402.14021), and its abridged version ``Scoring agents for what is neither verifiable nor falsifiable''.
  \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:consistency}} is based on: Daniel Paleka*, Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir*, Alejandro Alvarez, Vineeth Bhat, Adam Shen, Evan Wang and Florian Tram\`er, ``Consistency Checks for Language Model Forecasters'', ICLR 2025. My contributions centred on the theoretical framework (the arbitrage-based inconsistency metrics) and the \texttt{ArbitrageForecaster} method.
\end{itemize}
\end{draftblock}
